# Starting points by task

These are discovery routes, not current-version endorsements. Read [ADVISOR.md](ADVISOR.md), check the actual device and refresh the shortlist. Prefer existing compatible capabilities. Search commands retrieve original claims and dated reviews; they do not automatically decide what is best.

Use all rows that match parts of the job. For example, a realistic interactive website needs both the web-design and animation routes, plus asset/rendering specialists. Apply the advisor's coverage check before narrowing the result.

## Identity and evidence traps in this import

- `agent-skills` is generic and can refer to Addy Osmani or Vercel collections. Resolve owner/repo/subskill before comparing.
- `task-observer` appears in both task aggregation and learning-from-corrections narratives. Do not assume one verified tool supports both.
- Graphify/Graft/Graftv descriptions and quantified savings overlap; keep them distinct until verified upstream.
- Humanizer appears with `builder` and `blader` spellings. Apple Design is attributed to both Emil Kowalski and dickwu. Treat these as identity research tasks, not confirmed aliases.
- `Jarvis` is used for multiple projects and demos; the name alone cannot identify a repository.
- `latest_insight` has no per-claim timestamp. Entity ID/name variants and shared summaries are not safe automatic merge rules.
- Reported prices, star counts, model names, product announcements and claims of “official” status need primary-source checks. Conflicting free-vs-paid Open Generative AI descriptions are preserved, not resolved by choosing the more attractive claim.
- Trading, location investigation, security testing and food concepts are also in the source. Their presence does not make them general-purpose skills or justify recommending them for unrelated tasks.
