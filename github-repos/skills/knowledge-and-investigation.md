## [Geo-Sleuth](https://github.com/Oldcircle/geo-sleuth)

A specialized option for investigating locations in images using terrain, OCR, reverse-image search and ranked evidence.

Not a general research or SEO tool. It brings model/browser/data dependencies; some branches upload images to outside services. Its demonstrations do not establish accuracy on new images. Use only for an actual image-location task.

**Cost and license:** MIT code; data/image/model sources have separate terms and attribution. Model calls, compute, dependency/model downloads and optional services cost resources; no flat paid subscription is required by the core workflow.

## [Obsidian Skills](https://github.com/kepano/obsidian-skills)

Use if you keep an Obsidian vault. Markdown, Bases and JSON Canvas format guidance adds precise syntax beyond general writing.

Choose format skills first. CLI, Defuddle and Knap are separate optional dependencies; the CLI needs a running app and can write files or evaluate code. Your notes remain local and outside the setup backup.

**Cost and license:** MIT skills; no skill license fee. Codex/model usage remains. Optional external app/services retain their own licensing/pricing; Sync/Publish are not required by the format-editing skills.
